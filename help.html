Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Creating an account is easy! Just click on the "Sign Up" button on our homepage, enter your email address, create a password, and verify your email. Once verified, you can start completing tasks immediately.
I forgot my password. How can I reset it?
Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your registered email address, and we'll send you a password reset link. Check your email and follow the instructions to set a new password.
How do I make a deposit?
Go to the Deposit page, select your preferred plan, and use our M-Pesa Till Number: 9824375 (HAKIKA R PROVISION). After payment, paste the M-Pesa confirmation message, and your account will be activated immediately.
What payment methods do you accept?
We currently accept M-Pesa payments through our Till Number (9824375). We're working on adding more payment options including bank transfers and credit/debit cards in the near future.
How many tasks can I complete per day?
It depends on your plan: Basic (10 tasks/day), Standard (25 tasks/day), Premium & Ultimate (unlimited tasks). New tasks are added daily, and you'll see available tasks in your dashboard.
What happens if I fail a task?
If you fail a task (by providing inconsistent or low-quality answers), you won't earn the reward for that task. Your success rate will be affected, but you can continue with other tasks. We recommend taking your time and providing thoughtful answers.
How do I withdraw my earnings?
Go to the Earnings page and click "Withdraw". Enter the amount (minimum KSH 100) and your M-Pesa number. Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours on business days. A small transaction fee may apply.
What's the minimum withdrawal amount?
The minimum withdrawal amount is KSH 100. This helps us keep transaction costs low for everyone. You can accumulate your earnings until you reach this threshold.
